# CyberScan

CyberScan is a security intelligence platform for understanding application and infrastructure risk.

CyberScan is the current product name. ThreatGraph is the former project name.

CyberScan connects scanner evidence to application components, identities, resources, sensitive assets, attack paths, blast radius, and remediation priorities.

Scanner evidence and CyberScan analysis are distinct. Findings must be supported by evidence, and unknown relationships must remain explicitly uncertain.
